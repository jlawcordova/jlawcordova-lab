# Ambient loops and actions on the stage (spec R23, D10; plan step 27), on a
# temp copy of examples/harbor.pixellab: the dock barrel bobs on an ambient
# loop and the boat has a hop action. The boat also has scene motion, so the
# timeline is the longer of its motion and the barrel's loop.
appearance light
open scene:harbor
expect ambient 3000
# The barrel's loop: the timeline holds it.
select 68
expect actions none
# The boat: its hop in the Inspector, played once, mid-play held for a snapshot, then the rest frame.
select 41
expect actions hop
snapshot @OUT@/actions-inspector-light.png
play hop
expect lastplay hop:
expect playing none
playat hop 120
expect playing hop
snapshot @OUT@/actions-mid-play-light.png
playend
expect playing none
snapshot @OUT@/actions-rest-light.png
# Reduce Motion: Play shows the rest frame and says so.
reducemotion on
play hop
expect announced hop: shown at rest
expect playing none
reducemotion system
appearance dark
playat hop 120
snapshot @OUT@/actions-mid-play-dark.png
playend
